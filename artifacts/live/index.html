Do I need to keep my computer on?
No. Once your files are uploaded and the stream starts, YT Loop runs entirely in the cloud.
Can I stream multiple videos in a loop?
Yes. Create a playlist, choose a fixed or shuffled order, and YT Loop will repeat it continuously.
What happens if my stream disconnects?
Auto-Restart monitors the stream and reconnects it automatically, so downtime stays minimal.
Is it safe for monetized channels?
YT Loop is designed for legitimate creator-owned content. You remain responsible for YouTube policies and content rights.
Can I cancel anytime?
Absolutely. Plans are month-to-month and you can cancel before your next billing date.
